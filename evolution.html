The APEX Difference

Three Things PFM Modular Still Doesn't Solve

The modular deployment concept is proven and correct. APEX builds on that foundation and resolves the three remaining problems the industry has not addressed.

💧
The Water Problem
PFM Still Has: Water-cooled chillers on the module
APEX replaces the water-cooled chiller entirely with a dry air-cooled ammonia refrigeration system. Zero operational water consumption at any ambient temperature. The cooling tower that moved to the roof of the module — APEX eliminates it permanently. Not 80% less. Not minimal. Zero gallons. Always.
⚖️
The Regulatory Problem
PFM Still Has: HFC refrigerants facing AIM Act phase-out
Standard modular solutions use R-410A or R-32 — synthetic refrigerants that are being phased out under the AIM Act starting 2027. Every HFC-based modular unit installed today is a potential stranded asset within 5-10 years. APEX uses ammonia R-717 — GWP=0, ODP=0, no PFAS, no phase-out risk. Ever.
🧠
The Intelligence Problem
PFM Still Has: Reactive cooling with no GPU workload awareness
Every modular solution on the market — including PFM — cools reactively. It responds to heat after it happens. APEX Control reads GPU job schedulers and pre-cools before workloads ramp. By the time an AI training job hits full power, cooling is already at capacity. GPU throttle events eliminated. PUE 1.04-1.08 achieved.
