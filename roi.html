Understanding PUE

The Efficiency Gap That Costs Millions

PUE — Power Usage Effectiveness — measures how much total power goes to computing vs cooling overhead. Every point above 1.0 is money wasted on cooling, not computing.

At $0.07/kWh — every MW of PUE waste costs $613,000 per year in wasted electricity. The gap between PUE 1.5 and PUE 1.06 is 44MW — costing $27 million per year at 100MW scale.
Every MW of PUE Overhead Costs $613,000 Per Year.

Conventional cooling systems waste between 30% and 80% of their total power draw on cooling overhead — power that never reaches a GPU, never processes a training job, never generates revenue.

APEX Predictive Thermal Conditioning pre-cools the system before workloads ramp — meaning the refrigeration circuit never overworks to catch up. The result is a PUE of 1.04-1.08 — meaning 94-96 cents of every electricity dollar goes directly to compute.
