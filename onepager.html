The Problem

Texas — the #1 state for AI computing — is projected to consume 399 billion gallons of water annually for data center cooling by 2030, while simultaneously enduring historic multi-year drought. No state law limits data center water consumption.

The Solution

APEX MechPod — a containerized ammonia refrigeration system using dry air-cooled condensers. Zero operational water consumption. GWP=0. Deploys in days. Rated to 200 kW per rack. N+1 redundant. Patent Pending.

The Market

$27.65B liquid cooling market by 2033 at 31.5% CAGR. The addressable market for fully waterless, regulation-compliant AI cooling is currently unserved at commercial scale. APEX is first.
