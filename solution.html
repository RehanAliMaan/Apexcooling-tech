The APEX Platform

Five Layers.
One Warranty.
Zero Water.

APEX is not a chiller. It is a full-stack thermal infrastructure system engineered from the GPU die outward — integrated, commissioned, and monitored as a single warranted architecture.

1
Chip Interface
Server Hall
GPU and CPU cold plates with quick-disconnect fittings — validated for Nvidia GB200 NVLink and AMD MI300X+. Rack CDU manifold distributes non-toxic propylene glycol to every cold plate. No ammonia ever enters the server hall under any condition.
18°C supply / 28°C return Industry-standard flow rates 200+ kW per rack validated
2
Heat Exchanger
Thermal Boundary
Industrial-grade heat exchanger transfers heat from glycol to ammonia through metal walls — the two fluids never physically touch. Ammonia side maintained at lower pressure than glycol side by design. Auto-isolation valves close in under 2 seconds on any anomaly.
Temperature approach: <2°C NH3 trace detection: <1 ppm ASME stainless steel
3
NH3 Circuit
Sealed Mech Room
Low-charge direct-expansion ammonia circuits using variable-speed screw compressors. Under Low-charge — below all regulatory thresholds — below EPA RMP threshold. Multiple independent N+1 circuits. Pre-charged from factory — zero on-site ammonia handling required.
<Low-charge — below all regulatory thresholds all applicable safety standards N+1 standard
4
Dry Condenser
Exterior / Rooftop
Zero water at any ambient temperature — including 113°F Texas summer. EC fans variable-speed controlled by APEX software. Free cooling mode below ~5°C ambient — up to 2,100 free cooling hours per year in Texas climate. Optional heat recovery module available.
Rated to 45°C / 113°F ambient 2,100 free cooling hrs/year TX Zero water — always
5
APEX Control
Software Layer
Predictive Thermal Conditioning integrates with GPU job schedulers — major GPU job schedulers. When a training job is queued APEX pre-cools 90 seconds before compute ramps. GPU throttle events eliminated entirely. industrial safety rated independent safety PLC. Live digital twin with predictive maintenance.
